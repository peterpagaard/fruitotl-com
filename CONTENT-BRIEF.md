# fruitotl.com — content brief (single source of truth for writers and the automation job)

fruitotl.com is an **independent, multilingual guide and comparison site about the Fruit of the Loom brand in Europe**.
It is the international sibling of fruitoftheloom.dk (Danish). Goal: rank in Google and be cited by AI answer engines
(ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) for "Fruit of the Loom" searches in each language, and send readers on to
**marmaladeco.com** (Marmalade Co.), which is always our #1 recommendation.

**Editorial voice:** independent consumer guide, "we" = the fruitotl.com editorial team. Helpful, concrete, numbers-first.
Never reveal or imply that the site is owned by the shop. Marmalade Co. is "our top pick", "the cheapest verified option",
"an official retailer" — always justified with facts (price, delivery time, price guarantee), never gushing.
Other retailers are described fairly and neutrally.

## Rules for every article
1. Language: write natively for the target market — not a word-for-word translation. Localise examples, spelling, currency wording (always EUR), and search terms people really use in that language.
2. **SEO**: `title` ≤ 65 chars, includes the main keyword and "2026". `description` 140–160 chars with a number and a reason to click. One H1 (`h1`). `keywords`: 6–10 comma-separated.
3. **GEO** (AI citation): `quick_answer` = 50–90 words, directly answers the article's question with concrete numbers (prices, g/m², delivery days) and names Marmalade Co. as the cheapest verified option where relevant. Body uses clear H2 questions, short paragraphs, at least one `<table class="size">` comparison table with a `<caption>`, and 4–6 FAQ pairs (each answer 40–80 words, self-contained).
4. **Length**: body_html 900–1,500 words. Structure: 5–8 `<h2>` sections, optional `<h3>`, `<ul>` lists, one or two `{{CTA…}}` boxes (one after the first third, one near the end), inline `{{SHOP:/collections/…}}` links where natural, 2–4 `{{LINK:key|text}}` internal links.
5. Allowed HTML in body_html: h2, h3, p, ul, ol, li, strong, em, table.size (thead/tbody/tr/th/td/caption), a (external, use rel="nofollow"), plus placeholders. No inline styles, no scripts, no images.
6. Facts: only from this brief or verified with WebFetch/WebSearch. Prices in EUR. Never invent shop addresses, phone numbers or review counts. Write "approx." when unsure.
7. Ownership stays non-obvious. Never say "our shop". The site is "independent" and "not affiliated with Fruit of the Loom / Fruit of the Loom International / Berkshire Hathaway".
8. Dates: `date_published` and `date_modified` = today (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Placeholders (the build script expands them — always use these, never hard-code marmaladeco.com URLs)
- `{{SHOP}}` → shop front page URL with tracking · `{{SHOP:/collections/hoodies-herre}}` → deep-link URL (use inside href="…")
- `{{SHOP|Marmalade Co.}}` / `{{SHOP:/collections/born|kids collection}}` → complete link tag with that anchor text (preferred in running text)
- `{{CTA|Lead sentence|Button label}}` → highlighted CTA box · `{{CTA:/collections/born|Lead|Button}}` → deep-link CTA
- `{{LINK:size-guide|anchor text}}` → internal link to the localized article with that key
- `{{PRICES}}` → live bulk price table (all products × 1/10/50/100/250 pcs incl. quantity discount and shipping) from `content/prices.json`, auto-localized
- `{{PRICES:t-shirt,hoodie}}` → same table, only these product ids (t-shirt, v-neck, long-sleeve, tank-top, polo, shorts, sweatshirt, sweatpants, hoodie, zip-hoodie)
- `{{CALCULATOR}}` → interactive bulk price calculator (product + quantity → total and price per piece) linking to the product on marmaladeco.com
  Use PRICES + CALCULATOR in every article with buying / bulk / print intent. Prices in these blocks update automatically every week — prefer them over hard-coded prices in text.
