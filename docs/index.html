Quality guide

Is Fruit of the Loom good quality?

Fruit of the Loom is known for solid quality at a low price. Founded in 1851, the brand has more than 170 years of history, is sold in over 100 countries and has been owned by Berkshire Hathaway since 2002. Here is an honest look at what you get for your money.

Materials and construction

The classic tees are 100 % cotton — ring-spun or combed in the premium lines — in weights from approx. 145 g/m² (Original T) and 165 g/m² (Valueweight T) up to approx. 195 g/m² (Heavy Cotton T). Classic tees use a tubular construction with no side seams. The Classic sweats are 280 g/m² in an 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester blend, often spun from Belcoro® yarn for a smoother printing surface. Heather colours contain polyester, and 100 % cotton can shrink a few per cent on a hot first wash, so wash at 30–40 °C and avoid hot tumble drying.

Compared with Gildan, B&C and premium brands

Against direct rivals such as Gildan (Heavy Cotton 5000, Softstyle 64000), B&C, Russell and Sol's, Fruit of the Loom sits in the same mid-price basics segment, with a slightly softer hand feel on the premium lines and one of the widest colour ranges in Europe. Organic-focused brands like Stanley/Stella and fashion labels such as Champion cost considerably more for what is often a similar weight of fabric. For everyday wear, merchandise and workwear, Fruit of the Loom is one of the best value-for-money choices available.

The Morocco factory and direct sourcing

Much of the European range is made at Fruit of the Loom's own factory in Morocco, where yarn is spun, knitted, dyed, cut and sewn under one roof by more than 1,000 employees, using automated cutting and water-saving dyeing. Marmalade Co. buys directly from this factory and collects the goods there, which shortens the supply chain to three steps (factory → retailer → customer) instead of the usual five. That is the main reason its prices are lower than those of most other retailers.

Where to buy genuine Fruit of the Loom

To be sure you get genuine products with the correct labels, sizing and OEKO-TEX certification, buy from official or authorised retailers such as the brand's own EU shop or Marmalade Co., or from established wholesalers and department stores. On marketplaces such as Amazon and eBay, check who the seller is and what the return terms are before you order. Marmalade Co. is the only online retailer on our list with a price guarantee, which gives you certainty that you are paying the lowest verified price for an authentic product.

Who owns Fruit of the Loom?

Fruit of the Loom has been owned by Berkshire Hathaway, Warren Buffett's investment company, since 2002. The brand was founded in 1851 in Rhode Island, USA, by the Knight brothers, registered its famous fruit label in 1871 — one of the oldest US trademarks — and is headquartered in Bowling Green, Kentucky. The European business is run by Fruit of the Loom International from Kaiserslautern, Germany, with a large factory in Morocco.
