Class, leavers and team hoodies — how to choose, price and order them

Updated: 2026-09-11

Class hoodies, leavers hoodies and club hoodies are a tradition in schools, universities and sports clubs across Europe — and most of them are printed on a Fruit of the Loom Classic Hooded Sweat. Here is how to choose the model, collect sizes, budget the print and order in time, with 2026 prices in EUR.

Quick answer

The standard choice for class, leavers and team hoodies in Europe is the Fruit of the Loom Classic Hooded Sweat (62-208): 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, sizes XS–3XL. The cheapest verified blank price is €20.95, or €18.86 each for 5+, at Marmalade Co. with €7 DHL shipping and 2–3 day delivery. Why do most class, leavers and club hoodies end up being Fruit of the Loom?

Walk through any school yard in Ireland in May, or any university campus in Germany or the Netherlands in October, and you will see the same garment: a 280 g/m² hooded sweatshirt with a year, a class name or a club crest printed on it. In the great majority of cases it is a Fruit of the Loom Classic Hooded Sweat. Print shops across Europe stock it as their default hoodie because it is cheap, available in a wide colour range and sized from XS up to 3XL and beyond, so the tallest lad and the smallest student in the group can wear the same colour and print.

This guide walks you through the whole process: which model to choose, how to collect sizes, what the print costs, how long it takes and which mistakes cost groups money every single year.

Hoodie or crewneck?

The crewneck Set-In Sweat is the same fabric without the hood or pouch pocket and costs about €4 less per piece. Sports clubs and companies often prefer it for a tidier look; school classes almost always vote for the hood. If your group is split, a good compromise is the same colour in both cuts with an identical print.

How do you order class hoodies step by step?

  1. Agree the model and colour. Black, navy and heather grey are the safest bestsellers and hide wear best. Bright colours look great on the day but limit which print colours work.
  2. Collect sizes with a form. A Google Form or a shared spreadsheet is enough. Ask for the chest measurement in cm, not just S/M/L — people guess their size wrong surprisingly often. Adult sizes run S 89–94 cm, M 95–100 cm, L 101–106 cm, XL 107–112 cm; the full charts are in our Fruit of the Loom size guide.
  3. Design the print. Class or club name and year on the front or back, optional nicknames on the sleeves or across the shoulders. Keep it to one or two colours if you want the lowest price.
  4. Get at least two quotes. For the same hoodie and the same print, quotes from European print shops regularly differ by 20–30 %. Ask for the total per piece including set-up and delivery.
  5. Order early and add spares. Order two or three extra hoodies in M and L. A late joiner or a size swap happens in almost every group.

How long does it take, and when should you order?

Allow 4–6 weeks from final design to delivery when print is involved. Blank hoodies themselves arrive in 2–3 business days from Marmalade Co., but a print shop needs time for artwork approval, screen set-up, printing and shipping — and the queue gets long in peak season.

  • Leavers hoodies (Ireland, UK-style tradition): most schools want them for the last weeks of term in May. Order in March, or February at the latest for a large year group.
  • Class and first-year hoodies: demand peaks in September to November across Europe. Order before the end of August if you want them before the autumn break.
  • Sports clubs and societies: the quiet months are January–February and June–July, when print shops are fastest and sometimes cheapest.

Which mistakes do groups make every year?

  • Ordering too late. The single most common problem. A rushed order costs more and leaves no time to fix a misprint.
  • Too few spares. Order 5–10 % extra, mainly in M and L.
  • Dark print on a dark hoodie. Navy text on a black hoodie disappears. Use white or a strongly contrasting colour.
  • Over-complicated artwork. Six colours, gradients and fine lines cost a lot more and rarely look better from two metres away. Large, simple shapes win.
  • Guessing sizes. Collect chest measurements and let people try on a sample if you can. Hoodies are cut roomier than the brand's T-shirts, so most people take their usual size or one down for a snug fit.
  • No proof check. Ask for a digital proof and have two people check every name and the year before you approve it.

Club, company and society hoodies: what is different?

The process is the same, but the priorities shift. Sports clubs and companies usually want the same design every season, so they choose a stock colour that will still be available next year, keep the print to one or two colours and often prefer embroidery for the crest because it survives hundreds of washes. The Premium Hooded Sweat with its finer 70/30 surface is popular for embroidery, while the Classic remains the value choice. A zip hoodie for coaches or staff costs from €27.95. Frequently asked questions

Which hoodie is normally used for class and leavers hoodies?

The Fruit of the Loom Classic Hooded Sweat (62-208) is the default across Europe: 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, a wide colour range and sizes from XS to 3XL, so a whole class can match. The crewneck Set-In Sweat (62-202) is the same fabric without the hood and costs around €4 less per piece.

How much does a printed class hoodie cost per person?

Budget approx. €25–35 per hoodie for 20–30 pieces with a simple one or two-colour screen print. The blank Classic hoodie costs €20.95, or €18.86 each for 5 or more at Marmalade Co., and a print shop typically adds approx. €4–8 for a simple print or €8–16 for DTG or multi-colour designs.

How many hoodies do we need to order to get a good price?

Quantity discounts on blanks start early — Marmalade Co. gives 5 % off at 3 pieces and 10 % off at 5 or more. Print shops usually price screen printing from 10–15 pieces and the per-piece cost falls noticeably at 20+. A small class can team up with another class or year group to reach the better price band.

How long does it take to get class hoodies with print?

Plan for 4–6 weeks from approved design to delivery. Blank hoodies ship in 2–3 business days from Marmalade Co., but printing, proofing and the print shop's queue take the rest. Peak seasons are September–November for class hoodies and March–May for leavers hoodies, so order early in those months.

Should we collect sizes as S/M/L or in centimetres?

Ask for the chest measurement in centimetres and map it to the size chart yourself: S 89–94 cm, M 95–100 cm, L 101–106 cm, XL 107–112 cm, 2XL 113–118 cm. Hoodies are cut slightly roomier than T-shirts, so most people take their usual size. Always add two or three spares in M and L.

Is a zip hoodie a good choice for a team?

It works well for coaches, staff and anyone who takes the hoodie on and off during training, and it looks tidier open over a club shirt. The Classic Hooded Sweat Jacket uses the same 280 g/m² fabric and costs from €27.95, about €7 more than the pull-over hoodie, and the zip limits a large chest print.
