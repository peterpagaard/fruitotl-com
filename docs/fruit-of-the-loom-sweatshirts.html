Fruit of the Loom sweatshirts — the complete crewneck guide

Updated: 2026-09-11

A crewneck sweatshirt is the simplest garment in the Fruit of the Loom range: no hood, no pocket, no zip — just 280 g/m² of brushed fleece with a ribbed neck. That simplicity is exactly why it is so widely used for printing, workwear and everyday layering. This guide covers the models, the fit, the care and what a genuine Fruit of the Loom sweatshirt should cost in Europe in 2026.

What is a Fruit of the Loom sweatshirt?

In Fruit of the Loom's catalogue a "sweat" is any fleece-backed garment, and the crewneck is called a Set-In Sweat because the sleeves are set into the shoulder seam rather than cut raglan-style. The fabric is a cotton-rich fleece: smooth jersey on the outside, brushed loops on the inside for warmth. The crewneck is the most affordable way into that fabric, typically €4 cheaper than the hoodie made from the same cloth.

Fruit of the Loom sweatshirt models

Classic Set-In Sweat (62-202) — the standard

The Classic Set-In Sweat is 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester. It has a ribbed crew neck with a small V-insert, ribbed cuffs and waistband, and a regular fit that leaves room for a T-shirt underneath. Many colours are knitted from Belcoro® yarn, which gives an even surface for screen printing and DTG. This is the model most retailers, including Marmalade Co., sell simply as "crewneck sweatshirt".

Lightweight Set-In Sweat — the thinner version

A 240 g/m² variant built on the same pattern. It is the better choice for offices, warmer countries and anyone who wants a sweatshirt that does not feel like a winter garment. It is slightly less warm and slightly less opaque in light colours.

Premium Sweat — the polished option

A 70 % cotton / 30 % polyester blend with a finer, flatter surface. It is aimed at corporate wear and embroidery, where a smooth base makes logos look sharper. Expect a slightly higher price than the Classic.

Crewneck or hoodie — which should you choose?

The Classic Set-In Sweat and the Classic Hooded Sweat are made from the same 280 g/m² fabric, so the decision is about style and use rather than quality:

  • Choose the crewneck if you wear it under a coat or jacket (no hood bunching up), want a cleaner look for work, or want the largest uninterrupted print area on the chest and back.
  • Choose the hoodie if you want extra warmth around the neck, a kangaroo pocket, or the classic casual look that teenagers and sports clubs prefer.
  • Budget: the crewneck costs €16.95 against €20.95 for the hoodie at Marmalade Co. — a €4 difference that adds up quickly on a group order.

Fit and size guide for Fruit of the Loom sweatshirts

Sweatshirts are cut a little roomier than the brand's T-shirts. Most people take their normal size; for an oversized or layered look, go one size up. If you are between sizes, choose the larger one — cotton fleece can lose a little length in the first wash.

Popular colours and what they are used for

The crewneck is sold in around 10 colours at Marmalade Co. Black, navy and grey melange are the perennial bestsellers because they hide wear and suit any logo colour. White and light blue are popular for summer prints and fashion-style merch, while olive and dark green have become the go-to "neutral with character" for company wear. Note that melange (heather) shades contain more polyester than solid colours to produce the marbled effect.

Washing and durability

The 80/20 cotton-polyester blend is the reason these sweatshirts hold up so well: the polyester stabilises the fabric so it shrinks and twists less than pure cotton. To keep it that way:

  • Wash at 30–40 °C, inside out, with similar colours.
  • Air dry or tumble dry on low; high heat flattens the fleece and shrinks the ribbing.
  • Iron inside out on low, never directly on a print.

Frequently asked questions

What weight is the Fruit of the Loom crewneck sweatshirt?

The Classic Set-In Sweat (62-202) is 280 g/m² in an 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester blend, brushed on the inside. A Lightweight Set-In Sweat at approx. 240 g/m² and a Premium 70/30 version also exist, but the 280 g/m² Classic is the one most retailers stock.

How much does a Fruit of the Loom sweatshirt cost in Europe?

Most European marketplaces list the blank crewneck at €20–28. The cheapest verified price is Marmalade Co. at €16.95 incl. VAT, or €15.26 each for five or more, with €7 DHL shipping and delivery in 2–3 business days to Ireland and most EU countries.

Is a Fruit of the Loom sweatshirt warm enough for winter?

As a mid layer, yes. At 280 g/m² with a brushed inside it is as warm as the brand's hoodie, and it layers neatly under a coat because there is no hood. On its own it suits autumn and mild winter days; for cold outdoor use combine it with a jacket.

Do Fruit of the Loom sweatshirts shrink in the wash?

Very little, thanks to the 20 % polyester share that stabilises the fabric. Wash at 30–40 °C and avoid hot tumble drying and the sweatshirt keeps its size. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one, and wash printed sweatshirts inside out to protect the print.

Is the Fruit of the Loom crewneck good for printing?

Yes, it is one of the most printed sweatshirts in Europe. Many colours use Belcoro® yarn, which gives a smooth, even surface for screen printing and DTG, and the plain chest without a pocket offers the largest print area of any Fruit of the Loom sweat.
