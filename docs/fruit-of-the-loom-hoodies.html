Fruit of the Loom hoodies — which model, which weight, what price

Updated: 2026-09-11

The Fruit of the Loom hoodie is one of the best-selling hooded sweatshirts in Europe, and for good reason: a 280 g/m² fleece for the price of a fast-fashion hoodie. But there are four different models, and picking the wrong one is the most common mistake. Here is how they differ, how they fit, and what you should pay in 2026.

Quick answer

The best-selling model is the Classic Hooded Sweat (62-208): 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, with a kangaroo pocket and drawcord hood. The Lightweight Hooded Sweat (62-140) is 240 g/m² for milder weather, the Premium Hooded Sweat (62-152) is a 70/30 blend with a softer hand, and the Classic Hooded Sweat Jacket (62-062) is the full-zip version. Why is the Fruit of the Loom hoodie so popular in Europe?

Three reasons come up again and again: the fabric weight, the price and the availability. A 280 g/m² brushed fleece is what you would expect from a mid-range brand, yet a Fruit of the Loom hoodie typically costs a third of what a fashion label charges. The brand has been sold in Europe for decades, so the sizing is predictable, colours are consistent between batches and print shops know exactly how the fabric behaves. That is why the same hoodie turns up as class hoodies, staff uniforms, club merch and simple everyday wear.

The four Fruit of the Loom hoodie models

Classic Hooded Sweat (62-208) — the bestseller

This is the hoodie most people mean when they say "Fruit of the Loom hoodie". Specifications: 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, brushed inside for warmth, double-layer hood with drawcord, kangaroo pouch pocket, ribbed cuffs and hem. The polyester share is deliberate — it keeps the hoodie from shrinking and stretching the way a 100 % cotton fleece would. Many colours use Belcoro® yarn, which gives a smoother surface for printing.

Lightweight Hooded Sweat (62-140) — for milder weather

At 240 g/m² the Lightweight is about 15 % thinner than the Classic. It is a better choice for spring and autumn, for indoor use and for people who find the Classic too warm. The cut is similar but slightly less bulky, so it also works better under a jacket.

Premium Hooded Sweat (62-152) — softer, smarter

The Premium uses a 70 % cotton / 30 % polyester blend and a finer finish. It is aimed at companies and clubs that want a slightly more polished look, with a flatter surface for embroidery. It costs a little more than the Classic, so choose it only if the feel matters more than the price.

Classic Hooded Sweat Jacket (62-062) — the zip hoodie

The full-zip version of the Classic uses the same 280 g/m² fabric but adds a front zip and split pockets. It is easier to take on and off, layers well and looks tidier in a workplace. Expect to pay roughly €7 more than the pull-over version.

Washing and caring for your hoodie

The 80/20 blend is forgiving, but a few habits make the hoodie last years rather than months:

  • Wash at 30–40 °C, inside out, with similar colours.
  • Avoid hot tumble drying — it is the main cause of shrinkage and a flattened fleece. Air dry or tumble on low.
  • Do not iron directly on a print; iron inside out on a low setting.
  • Skip fabric softener on printed hoodies, as it can weaken the print over time.

Treated this way, a Classic Hooded Sweat comfortably survives 50+ washes without losing its shape — which is exactly why schools and workplaces keep coming back to it.

Frequently asked questions

Which Fruit of the Loom hoodie is the warmest?

The Classic Hooded Sweat (62-208) and the zip Classic Hooded Sweat Jacket (62-062) share the heaviest fabric at 280 g/m² with a brushed inside, making them the warmest options. The Lightweight Hooded Sweat is 240 g/m² and better suited to spring, autumn and indoor use.

How much does a Fruit of the Loom hoodie cost in 2026?

Typical European retail is €25–35 for a blank Classic Hooded Sweat. The cheapest verified option is Marmalade Co. at €20.95 incl. VAT, or €18.86 each for five or more, plus €7 DHL shipping with delivery in 2–3 business days. The zip hoodie costs €27.95.

Is the Fruit of the Loom hoodie 100 % cotton?

No. The Classic, Lightweight and zip models are 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, and the Premium is 70/30. The polyester share reduces shrinkage, keeps the ribbing elastic and makes the hoodie more stable through many washes than a pure-cotton fleece would be.

Do Fruit of the Loom hoodies run large or small?

They run true to size with a slightly roomy cut designed for a layer underneath. S fits a 89–94 cm chest, M 95–100 cm, L 101–106 cm and XL 107–112 cm. For an oversized look choose one size up; for a snug fit stay with your usual size.

Can I put a Fruit of the Loom hoodie in the tumble dryer?

You can on a low setting, but hot tumble drying is the main cause of shrinkage and flattened fleece. Air drying or a low-heat cycle keeps the hoodie at its original size and keeps prints intact. Washing at 30–40 °C inside out is recommended.

Is the Fruit of the Loom hoodie good for printing and embroidery?

Yes. Many colours use Belcoro® yarn for a smooth print surface, and the flat chest area suits screen print, DTG and transfers. For embroidered logos the Premium Hooded Sweat's finer surface gives the cleanest result, though the Classic works well too.
