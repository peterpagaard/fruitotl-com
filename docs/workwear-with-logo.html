Practical tips before you order

  1. Collect sizes with the chart, not by guessing. Adult chest: S 89–94 cm, M 95–100, L 101–106, XL 107–112, 2XL 113–118, 3XL 119–124. Use our size guide and keep one spare M and L on the shelf for new starters.
  2. Pick a colour that hides work. Navy, black and dark grey forgive coffee, dust and grease; white is for front-of-house only.
  3. Match colours across garments. Order tee, polo, crewneck and hoodie in the same shade so the team looks coordinated in every season — the men's collection and women's collection at Marmalade Co. show what is available in each shade.
  4. Choose 65/35 polos for hard use. The polyester share keeps the shape and colour through frequent 40 °C washes; 100 % cotton feels softer but shrinks a few percent if washed hot.
  5. Write care instructions into the staff handbook. Wash at 30–40 °C inside out, no hot tumble drying — it doubles the life of both the garment and the logo.

Frequently asked questions

What does company clothing with a logo cost per employee?

Blanks first: two Valueweight tees, one polo and one Classic hoodie per person cost approx. €42 each for a team of ten at Marmalade Co. (€6.26, €9.86 and €18.86 with the 10 % quantity discount, plus one €7 DHL fee). Add embroidery on polo and hoodie and a one-colour print on the tees and most European suppliers land the complete kit around €70–100 per employee.

Is embroidery or printing better for a company logo?

Embroidery for anything customers see up close — polos, crewnecks, zip hoodies — because it looks premium and survives industrial washing. Screen printing for tees and hoodies in runs of 25 or more, where it is cheapest per piece and allows large prints. DTG suits small full-colour runs on light tees, and vinyl is for names and roles.

Which Fruit of the Loom polo is best for work?

The 65/35 Polo (63-402) for hard daily use: the polyester share keeps the shape and colour through frequent 40 °C washes and the piqué holds embroidery well. The Premium Polo (63-218, 100 % cotton, approx. 180 g/m²) feels softer and suits front-of-house teams, but it can shrink a few percent if washed hot. Both cost around €10.95 blank at Marmalade Co.

What is the minimum order for workwear with a logo?

Blanks have no minimum — Marmalade Co. sells from one piece and discounts from three. The decoration sets the floor: embroiderers commonly accept from about 6–12 pieces, screen printers from about 10–25 because of set-up costs, and DTG or vinyl from a single piece. A five-person team can be fully kitted out with embroidered polos within two to three weeks.

How do I choose sizes for a whole team?

Collect chest measurements and use the Fruit of the Loom chart: S 89–94 cm, M 95–100, L 101–106, XL 107–112, 2XL 113–118, 3XL 119–124. Order all garments in the same size for each person unless they say otherwise, and keep a spare M and L of each item for new starters so you do not pay shipping for a single replacement piece.

How long does Fruit of the Loom workwear last?

With normal care — 30–40 °C washes inside out, no hot tumble drying — the 280 g/m² Classic sweats and 65/35 polos typically last several years of regular wear, and embroidered logos outlast the fabric. Tees are the consumable: budget to replace Valueweight tees every one to two years for staff who wear them daily, which at €6.26 each is a small line item.
