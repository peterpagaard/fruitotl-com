What are Fruit of the Loom sweatpants?

Sweatpants, joggers, jog pants, track pants — the names vary by country, but they all describe the same thing: fleece-backed trousers with an elastic waist. Fruit of the Loom's versions use the same 280 g/m² brushed fleece as the Classic Hooded Sweat and Classic Set-In Sweat, so the colours and the feel match if you combine them into a set. They are simple, warm and inexpensive, which is why they are used for everything from gym bags and lounging to school PE kits and printed team sets.

The two models: elasticated cuff vs open hem

Classic Elasticated Cuff Jog Pants (64-026)

The cuffed model has ribbed ankle cuffs that keep the leg in place. It is the more popular of the two in Europe and the one most people picture when they think of joggers: a slightly tapered look, no fabric dragging on the ground, and it stays put when you push it up over the calf. Specifications: 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, elasticated waist with drawcord, two side pockets, ribbed cuffs.

Classic Open Hem Jog Pants (64-032)

The open-hem model has a straight, uncuffed leg. It looks more like a relaxed trouser than a jogger, works better with taller people who find cuffed joggers too short, and is often preferred for workwear and by older buyers. Same fabric, same waistband and pockets — only the hem differs.

Which sweatpants should you choose?

  • Cuffed if you want the classic jogger look, plan to train or run in them, or are ordering for teenagers and sports clubs.
  • Open hem if you want a straighter, trouser-like leg, are tall, or need something that reads as workwear rather than sportswear.
  • Unsure? Cuffed is the safer all-round choice and the more widely stocked of the two.

Fruit of the Loom sweatpants size guide

Jog pants are sized S–2XL (some retailers also stock XS and 3XL) and follow the waist rather than the chest. The elasticated waist with drawcord is forgiving, so most people take the same letter size as their hoodie. Leg length is standard; tall wearers over about 190 cm may prefer the open-hem model.

Washing and durability

The 80/20 blend keeps the sweatpants stable through many washes, but the ribbed cuffs and waistband are the parts most affected by heat. Wash at 30–40 °C, inside out, and avoid hot tumble drying so the elastic keeps its stretch and the fleece stays soft. Do not iron over a print. Expect a pair to last several years of regular use; the knees are typically the first area to show wear on cuffed models used for sport.

Sweatpants for teams, schools and workwear

Plain jog pants with a small printed or embroidered logo on the thigh are a common choice for sports clubs, school PE kits and staff at gyms and warehouses. The flat front thigh prints well, and the low unit price makes them viable even for large groups. Cuffed models are the norm for sport; open-hem models suit workplaces where a tidier trouser look is preferred.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Fruit of the Loom cuffed and open hem jog pants?

Only the hem. The Classic Elasticated Cuff Jog Pants (64-026) have ribbed ankle cuffs for a tapered jogger look; the Classic Open Hem Jog Pants (64-032) have a straight, uncuffed leg that looks more like a relaxed trouser. Both are 280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester, with a drawcord waist and side pockets.

Are Fruit of the Loom sweatpants warm?

Yes. At 280 g/m² with a brushed inside they are the same weight as the Classic hoodie, which makes them warm enough for autumn and winter lounging, training in cool weather and layering under outdoor trousers. In summer heat they are too warm; the brand's shorts are the lighter alternative.

Do Fruit of the Loom sweatpants shrink?

Only slightly, thanks to the 20 % polyester share. Wash at 30–40 °C and avoid hot tumble drying and they keep their length and the cuffs keep their stretch. If you are between sizes, take the larger one — the drawcord waist will hold it in place.
