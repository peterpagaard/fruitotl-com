How to split sizes in a bulk order

The best size split is the one you collect from the people who will wear the hoodies. Ask for chest measurements or let people try a sample, then order to that list. If you have to guess — for example for merch or spares — a common starting point for a mixed adult group is:

  • S: approx. 10–15 %
  • M: approx. 25–30 %
  • L: approx. 25–30 %
  • XL: approx. 15–20 %
  • XXL–XXXL: approx. 5–10 %

This is a rule of thumb, not a statistic: a youth club skews smaller, a construction crew larger. Add two or three spares in M and L for late sign-ups. Fruit of the Loom hoodies are a regular unisex fit; the chest in cm runs roughly S 89–94, M 95–100, L 101–106 and XL 107–112. The size guide has the full chart and a note on shrinkage.

Colours: what to check before ordering 50+ pieces

  • Stay within one model. The quantity discount applies per product, and you can mix colours and sizes of the same hoodie to reach 5 pieces.
  • Heather colours contain more polyester. Heather grey is popular and hides wear, but prints and dyes behave slightly differently than on solid colours.
  • Check stock per size. For large orders, confirm that every size you need is in stock in your colour before paying — especially XS and XXXL.
  • Plan reorders. Note the exact model and colour name. A reorder of the same model gives the most consistent shade.

How long does a bulk hoodie order take?

Blank hoodies from Marmalade Co. are dispatched within 0–1 business day and arrive in 2–3 business days by DHL, with tracking by e-mail. If the hoodies are going to a printer, add the printer's lead time — usually one to two weeks for screen printing or embroidery, sometimes longer in the busy weeks before school leavers' events and Christmas. For a deadline, work backwards: event date, minus printing time, minus a few days buffer, minus delivery. Our guide to class and team hoodies has a full ordering timeline.

Blank or printed: how much does printing add?

Many buyers order blank hoodies and bring them to a local printer, because it lets them choose the cheapest garment route and the printer separately. Printing costs depend on the method, the number of colours and the quantity: screen printing gets cheap per piece from a few dozen hoodies upward, embroidery suits small logos on staff hoodies, and DTG or vinyl works for small runs and individual names. Ask the printer whether they accept supplied garments and whether they charge a handling fee. See our guide to clothing for printing for which technique fits which job.

Checklist for ordering hoodies in bulk

  1. Choose one model — usually the Classic Hooded Sweat, 280 g/m².
  2. Collect sizes from the wearers and add a few spares in M and L.
  3. Choose colours and check stock per size before paying.
  4. Check the deadline against delivery (2–3 business days) plus printing time.
  5. Compare prices on the same basis: VAT included, shipping to your address.
