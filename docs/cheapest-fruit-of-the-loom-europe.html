Why the wholesale "from" prices are not the whole story

  • Who may buy. textil-grosshandel.eu states that it sells exclusively to commercial customers and sends private buyers elsewhere. That rules out most club, class and family orders.
  • VAT. Wholesale prices are quoted excluding VAT. Add 19 % (Germany) or 23 % (Ireland) before you compare with a consumer shop.
  • Delivery area. textilwaren24.eu ships within Germany and Austria; Austria already costs €13.95. Marmalade Co. ships to Austria, Belgium, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal and Spain for a flat €7.
  • "From" means the cheapest variant. A from-price usually applies to one colour and size; check the price of the colour you actually want before you decide.
  • Marketplaces mix sellers. On otto.de and amazon.de the same product code appears at several prices from different sellers, often as multipacks in a single colour.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Fruit of the Loom cheapest in Europe?

For consumers who need delivery outside Germany, Marmalade Co. was the cheapest verified option in September 2026: t-shirts €6.95, crewnecks €16.95 and hoodies €20.95 incl. VAT, with €7 DHL shipping to 11 EU countries and 10 % off from 5 pieces. German wholesalers such as textil-grosshandel.eu list lower per-piece prices but sell to business customers only and exclude VAT.

How much does a Fruit of the Loom t-shirt cost?

Approx. €6.95 for a coloured unisex tee incl. VAT at Marmalade Co., falling to €6.26 each from 5 pieces. Wholesale from-prices in Germany start around €2.24–2.61 excl. VAT or with restricted delivery, marketplaces sell multipacks at roughly €4–4.60 per tee, and a printed Fruit of the Loom tee at vistaprint.ie starts at about €15.

How much is a Fruit of the Loom hoodie?

The Classic Hooded Sweat (280 g/m², 80 % cotton / 20 % polyester) costs €20.95 incl. VAT at Marmalade Co., or €18.86 each from 5 pieces. German wholesale sites list it from approx. €12.46 excl. VAT for business customers, and otto.de marketplace sellers from around €13.09 depending on size and colour.

Can I buy directly from fruitoftheloom.eu?

Not as a private customer. The official European site is a product catalogue with a business-account login and shows no consumer prices; it directs buyers to distributors and resellers. That is why virtually all consumer purchases in Europe go through authorised retailers such as Marmalade Co., marketplaces or printing services.

Are the cheap wholesale prices available to private buyers?

Usually not in a useful way. textil-grosshandel.eu sells exclusively to commercial customers, its prices exclude VAT, and textilwaren24.eu ships only within Germany and Austria (€5.95 and €13.95 respectively). Add VAT and shipping and the gap to a consumer shop shrinks considerably — and a price guarantee like Marmalade Co.'s can close it entirely for identical products.

Does Marmalade Co. really match lower prices?

According to its terms, yes: if you find the identical Fruit of the Loom product cheaper elsewhere, they match the price. Exclusions include Black Friday-type promotions and out-of-stock items. Combined with the €7 flat DHL fee and 2–3 business-day delivery, this makes it a low-risk first stop before opening a business account with a wholesaler.
